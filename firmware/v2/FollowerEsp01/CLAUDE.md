# v2 FollowerEsp01 (ESP-01 row board, #298; wall link #559)

Loaded when working in this tree; root CLAUDE.md holds build commands, workflow and Hard rules. Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-wall-link-and-console-design.md`.

ESP8266 firmware turning v1 master hardware (ESP-01 + Nano row) into a row of a Split-Flap under one S3 master. Plain `.cpp` on the unified ESP32Async stack (#356: `esp32async/ESPAsyncWebServer` at the SAME exact version as Master/Rescue + `esp32async/ESPAsyncTCP` as its ESP8266 TCP side, `eagle.flash.1m.ld`); single-core superloop — **web handlers stage, `loop()` mutates and is the only I2C toucher** (`SERIAL_ENABLE` in `FollowerConfig.h` trades I2C for serial, shared pins `Wire.begin(1, 3)`).

- **Everything the master asks of this row arrives over the wall link** (`FollowerLink.cpp`, below). The HTTP routes are the three the board needs without a master, and nothing else (`tests/test_row_routes.py` pins the set; rescue mode serves the same): `POST /firmware/master?md5=` (ESP8266 Update flow, the recovery path; ota-flash.sh's rev compare is the revert detector), `GET /settings` (identity: `name`, `version`, `plat:"esp01"`, `width`, `rescue`, `master`, `masterHost`, `linked`, `up`, `heap`, `sketch`, `sketchFree`, `flashMode`, `flashId` — pure `FollowerJson.h`; ota-flash.sh reads `version` and `plat`) and `POST /pair`. The WiFi setup portal registers its own. No HTML, no CORS; a POST with an `Origin` that is not the board itself is refused (shared `LanOrigin.h`).
- **Pairing:** a master posts its id to `POST /pair` (form field `master`; its address is the caller's). Pure `FollowerPairPolicy.h`: an unpaired row stores it; its own master may repeat it (nothing changes); another master is refused (409 with the present master's id) unless the row has written its own off (LeaderLost) — a replaced master needs no step on the row; its own master's id from another address is taken only once the master is out of contact (25 s), so knowing the id does not move a working row. The reply is the identity document. The handler only stages; `loop()` writes the record. A row is freed by its master's `Release`.
- Phases (the shared `ClusterMemberPhase.h`; `FollowerPolicy.h` only adds what this row does with it): Standalone (unpaired) → Clustered → Grace (25 s without the master; holds the last text) → LeaderLost = **blank** or the fallback (120 s). The pairing — the master's id, address and tz rule — persists in EEPROM (`FollowerSettings.h`, magic `4FFS`; 42 bytes of the record are written as zero and stay, so the fields and the prefs record behind it keep their place across images) and a restart lands in Grace. SNTP epoch-only for flip instants; unsynced = render on arrival.
- **Fallback (#342):** the master's POSIX tz arrives in `Config` and persists with the pairing; a row that wrote its master off shows centered HH:MM, the date, or nothing, as `Config.fallback` says (pure `FollowerClock.h`; needs synced time and a zone). The master's content replaces it; `Release` drops the zone.
- **Rescue mode (#343):** RTC-memory bad-boot counter (pure `FollowerRescue.h`, word offset 32; zeroed by 60 s uptime or the deliberate-reboot path — which an installed image and the master's `Restart` take; a power cycle wipes it). 3 consecutive early deaths boot a minimal image: WiFi, the wall link, the download and the web routes only, no I2C/probe/boot-home/render, unit jobs refused, `rescue` in `Hello` → the master offers its stored image.
- **Boot-reflash brake (#513):** `reflashOnBoot` (default true; `Config.update_units_at_start`) gates BOTH boot passes — the early auto-install and the settled install + auto-update — so a gated campaign flashes nothing until asked. Persisted with the fallback choice in a 3-byte prefs record directly behind the pairing (`FollowerSettings.h`; an unreadable record decodes to the defaults, never to off). Glue `FollowerPrefs.cpp` (staged when `Config` arrives, `loop()` writes, commits spaced 10 s, flushed before a restart). The flash loop selects through `reflashShouldFlashUnit`. `tests/test_follower_boot_brake.py` pins the glue.
- **Reset history (#503):** each boot pushes the SDK reset info into a 4-entry RTC ring (pure `FollowerResetLog.h`, word offset 40, wiped by a power cycle) and logs this boot and the ones before it (`reason:exccause:epc1:excvaddr`) — the log is where the history is read. Log-ring lines carry an `[uptime s]` stamp.
- **WiFi join retry (#524):** `wifiInit()` gives the stored credentials `FOLLOWER_JOIN_ATTEMPTS` (3) join windows, in place, before the 5-minute setup portal; never `WiFi.disconnect()` between them (it can erase the SDK-persisted credentials). `tests/test_follower_join_retry.py` pins the loop.
- **WiFi TX power ladder (#508):** the shared `WifiTxPolicy.h` (same ladder as the S3, Master CLAUDE.md) run by `FollowerWifi.cpp` — stepped in the join loop, fixed for the portal, 1 Hz from `loop()`; an up-step polls the row for motion only when one is due. "Traffic confirmed" (#515) here = Clustered with fresh contact from the master (`clusterLeaderContactFresh`), so a row its master is talking to does not climb on a weak signal estimate. A firmware upload freezes the ladder and caps the level at the join ceiling. `Status.tx_power`. No level read-back exists on the ESP8266.
- Pure logic comes from `firmware/v2/shared/` (#408) — nothing to keep in sync. That includes the twiboot wire client (`TwibootFlash.h`, #526): `FollowerBus.cpp` supplies the `WireTwibootBus` adapter and the orchestration only (PROGMEM page fetch, `flashError` text from flash, batch settle). The op validators, the op / self-test result vocabulary and the reflash plan (incl. the consecutive-failure halt) are the shared `MaintenancePolicy.h` / `ReflashPlan.h` (#527); `FollowerOps.h` holds only the staged-job vocabulary and the unit facts buffer sizing; the slot itself and its drain are `FollowerUnitJobs.cpp`. Op grading (`maintGrade*`), the self-test wait (`SelfTestPoll.h`: stale-terminal rule, unsupported after 3 unreadable polls, 45 s re-armed on RUNNING — the op result is stamped when the test ends, ok = passed, as on the S3), the in-place fact patches / read invalidation and the in-system twiboot update (`BootUpdateOp.h`, hooks in `FollowerBus.cpp`) are shared too (#529/#530). The reflash batch size is a build flag (`REFLASH_BATCH_SIZE=2` — bench-tuned, NOT the S3's 4). The same-origin rule on the two POST routes is the shared `LanOrigin.h` (#531). **Unit bus (#534):** everything this row says to a unit is the shared `UnitBusCore.h` (frames, reads + validation, verified writes, the probe/poll reads, per-unit error ledger) and `UnitBusTwiboot.h` (rescue probe, boot-section read, unit flash), run on the `WireTwibootBus` adapter in `FollowerBus.cpp`; the flash loop is the shared `reflashRunTargets`, the boot dump `BootDumpOp.h`. `FollowerBus.cpp` keeps the adapter, the superloop scheduling, motion admission, bus-death recovery and log text (F() strings). Since the move this row also reads the drift diagnostics (`de/ds/dp/phys`), charges failed status reads and render writes to their unit (`err/errAge`), and counts `i2cTx/i2cErr` like the S3. Deliberately NOT here: the S3's per-unit transition log (`UnitEventLog.h` — a 4 KB log ring), a /stop abort of a flash, the S3's canary and phantom-ACK probe checks (ESP32 driver quirks). `POST /firmware/master` decides through the shared `OtaUploadGate.h` (#532): hex-validated md5 before `Update.begin`, and 409 while a unit or bootloader update is queued or running (`unitUpdateQueuedOrRunning`). Unit waits (probe inhibit, pre-probe delay, twiboot startup, return/home/stuck timeouts) are named once in `shared/UnitTimings.h` (#536). An op that invalidates a unit's reads (reboot-to-bootloader, boot dump, boot update) queues a probe — nothing else re-reads the offset here. `tests/test_shared_logic.py` (repo root) forbids re-implementing shared logic in a tree.
- `build_assets.py` bakes `data/unit-firmware.hex` (staged by `make_manifest.py stage`, CI-gated) into `UnitAssets.h` and stamps `follower-<rev>.bin` — the prefix ota-flash.sh keys the platform on.
- **OTA image size (#540):** one app area of 1,028,096 B (`eagle.flash.1m.ld`) holds the running image AND the stored upload; eboot copies the upload down at reboot. A plain image must fit beside itself (ceiling 510,976 B); the build's `follower-<rev>-gz.bin` is the same image gzip-packed, which eboot unpacks, and moves the ceiling to ~600 KB at today's 70 % ratio. Every build prints both margins and fails when the image could no longer replace itself (`buildtools/fwbuild.py`, `ota_*`). The core cannot check a gzip upload, so `POST /firmware/master` does (pure `FollowerOtaImage.h`): the image header must be readable inside the file (the build leaves the first 16 bytes uncompressed — a plain `gzip` of the bin is refused) and carry the flash mode and size the board runs; the upload is stored at the top of the app area; and the unpacked image must end below its stored copy — eboot does not notice when it overwrites input it has not read. A plain upload takes the core's path unchanged. `GET /settings` reports `sketch`, `sketchFree`, `flashMode` and `flashId` (the flash chip — only the existing row's chip is proven for a packed flash; the core has open Puya/XMC reports). The packed image is the default OTA artifact (`ota-flash.sh` prefers it); the plain one is for USB provisioning and a row still on v1. `tests/test_ota_gzip_eboot.py` runs the installed core's own eboot copy routine on the host against the packed image.
- **RAM discipline (#519):** 82 KB total, ~41 KB static. String literals live in RAM on this chip, and a `String`/`char*` body is copied again by the web library — so the one JSON the board serves is small and built in one reserved `String`, and everything large goes up the link from a buffer of `FollowerMem.h`. A throwing allocation resets this board on a miss, so a large buffer is asked for through `followerBufAlloc` (nullptr, never a throw). **Second heap (#564):** the image is built with `PIO_FRAMEWORK_ARDUINO_MMU_CACHE16_IRAM48_SECHEAP_SHARED` — the code memory it leaves unused (about 19.7 KB) is a second heap, at the price of half the flash-code cache. Only `FollowerMem.cpp` selects it (`followerBufAlloc`: second heap, else the first with its margin); it holds the log ring (claimed at the first line logged, 4.1 KB for good), the unit facts document on its way up the link and the boot-dump block, the last two only while in use. A byte access there costs about 3 µs and the SDK never allocates from it, so nothing a hot path walks byte by byte goes there (the link's message structs stay in fixed memory); `Status.heap2` = its free bytes. `tests/test_follower_second_heap.py` pins the flag and the three users. Scan lines for units that are not current: `FollowerScanLog.h`.
- **Time (#566):** SNTP with the master's address as first server and the public pool second (a row without its master, or unpaired); the zone is the master's. `configTime` restarts SNTP and runs from `loop()` only — a pairing or zone change marks the setup due (`FollowerCluster.cpp`).
- **Wall link (#564):** a paired row dials its master on TCP 7411 (`FollowerLink.cpp`, polled from `loop()`; timing rules `FollowerLinkPolicy.h`) and takes orders only from the master whose `Welcome` carries the paired id. `Show`/`Quiet`/`Ping`/`Release` drive `FollowerCluster.cpp` (phase, flip timing, fallback); `Config` → the prefs record (`fallback` blank/time/date in flag bits 1–2 stored +1 so an older record reads Time, `reflashOnBoot`) and the stored tz; `Op` → `FollowerLinkOps.h` (which shared check each `OpCode` takes, refusal named) → the one staged slot (`unitOpStage` in `FollowerUnitJobs.h`). One job of the master's at a time; its end is read from the result slot and sent as `OpState` (self-test and boot-info JSON in `data`, a boot dump as three pieces), also after a reconnect. `Status` every 10 s (`busy` follows the job slot). The unit facts document (the shared `buildUnitHealthJson`, offset included as `ofs`, plus wear and update progress) goes up as `UnitsJson` on connect, after a job and every 30 s, one piece per loop pass from a buffer that is given back when a job starts. `LogCtl` on → the log ring goes up as `LogLine`, one whole line per loop pass (`FollowerLogRing::nextLine`); the cursor outlives a connection, so lines logged while the master was away follow the ones it had. `Restart` restarts the board. A write that fails closes the connection. Bench master: `link/fake_master.py`.
- **Row firmware over the link (#564):** `Update` (rev, size, md5, packed, port) is taken up after the messages in hand are read: refused at once (`FollowerUpdatePolicy.h`: same rev unless in rescue mode, a unit job running, no rev/size/md5, larger than the free area) or fetched with `GET http://<master>:<port>/firmware/row` by `FollowerUpdate.cpp`, which blocks `loop()` for the whole download — no unit work, as during an upload. The master's answer (200, `Content-Length` equal to the offered size) and the first bytes (the `FollowerOtaImage.h` checks; the packed flag must match the file) are judged before `Update.begin` erases anything; the flash session is the upload route's (same reserve, same unpack check, md5 through the updater). `UpdateState` goes up before the silence (`DOWNLOADING`) and after it (`INSTALLED` → restart through the deliberate-reboot path, `FAILED`/`REFUSED` with a reason and detail); an answer that did not get out is sent on the next connection. One updater, one writer: `POST /firmware/master` answers 409 from the start of a download until the restart, and an upload in progress freezes `loop()` before the link is read. **Rescue mode runs the link** (Hello carries `rescue`): unit jobs are refused, no unit facts are sent, and the offered image is taken even at the running rev.
- Tests: `pio test -e native` (policy/settings/json/pairing/link/update/log/clock/rescue) + `uv run --with pytest python -m pytest tests/` (source gates, the boot copier harness).
- **Escalation (#503):** a fault that does not heal ends in a rate-limited self-restart — rules, limits and the RTC record in `FollowerEscalation.h`; the count is `Status.escalations`.
- **A dead unit bus (#488/#496):** detection and backoff are pure `FollowerBusRecovery.h`. A row is empty, and so dead and probed again at every recovery attempt, while none of its units has ANSWERED (a bootloader's identity, a version or a status read — `FollowerBusDeath.h`); an address that only acknowledged at a scan does not make it a row. Once per episode, at its first attempt, the row writes down what the bus looks like (line state, how many of the 16 addresses acknowledge, how long since its last move) and how the two lines rise with only what is outside the chip pulling them up (`FollowerLineTest.cpp`, measured every 10 min on the working bus for comparison): four log lines and the events `ROW_EVT_BUS_DEAD` / `ROW_EVT_BUS_LINES`. `tests/test_bus_and_upload_recovery_glue.py` pins the glue.
- **Events for the master (#570):** the start with its reset cause, a start that follows a self-restart, and memory running low wait in a four-place queue (pure `FollowerEvents.h`; the newer one goes when it is full) and are sent as `Event` once the master is reached, each with how long ago it was. Codes are `RowEventCode` in `wall_link.proto`; a code keeps its meaning for good, the master stores the number.
