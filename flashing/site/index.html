Split-Flap

Looking for the latest release…

Install the firmware on a new board from this page. It needs Chrome or Edge on a computer: the browser talks to the board over USB.

This browser cannot reach a USB serial port. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Master

ESP32-S3 with 16 MB flash. Plug its USB port into this computer. This writes the whole flash: bootloader, firmware and the rescue image.

This page must be opened over HTTPS.

Row board

ESP-01 with 1 MB flash, in a USB programmer that starts it in flash mode (GPIO0 held low while it powers up).

This page must be opened over HTTPS.

The installer reads which board is connected and takes the image made for it, so a row board is never given the master's firmware or the other way round.

What next

  1. A new board opens its own WiFi network, named after the board and ending in -setup. Join it and give the board your WiFi name and password.
  2. Open the master in a browser, by the name it shows on your network (split-flap-xxxxxx.local) or by its address.
  3. In Wall settings on the master, add the row boards: the master finds them on the network and pairs with them.

A wall that already runs finds new releases by itself and installs one from its Firmware screen; it does not need this page.
